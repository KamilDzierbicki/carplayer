How it works
  1. Scan the QR code, or open its link on a laptop with Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
  2. Click "Share my screen" and pick a screen, window or tab.
  3. The picture appears here, drawn on a canvas like every video. Tab audio comes along when the browser allows it.
  • Phones cannot share their screen from a browser. A phone can pass the link on to a laptop.
  • The link is valid for a few minutes; the QR code renews itself.

CarPlayer

Video, TV and radio for passengers that keeps playing while you drive.

Terms

Your data stays in this browser CarPlayer stores nothing on its server. Settings, history, keys and tokens stay here. The relay only passes short messages from your phone, in memory, for up to 3 minutes.
You bring the sources Video links, Jellyfin, IPTV lists, radio links and the proxy come from you. You are responsible for having the right to watch them.
For passengers only The driver must not watch video while driving.
Made with <3 from Poland by kamilmlody5

Settings are saved in this browser. Copy them to a phone, or bring them from a phone, with a QR code. History is not copied.

Export to phone

Scan with the phone camera. The phone opens CarPlayer and asks before it saves the settings.

Import from phone

Scan on a phone that already has CarPlayer set up, then tap "Send settings to car".
